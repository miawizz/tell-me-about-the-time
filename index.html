HOW TO PLAY

One person says, “[Name]... tell me about the time...” and taps the button to finish the sentence with a wild, imaginative prompt that is clearly not real.

The other person immediately jumps into telling the story, fully committing to the idea using that “Yes, And” guiding improv principle.

The listener can ask questions along the way to help the story grow!
