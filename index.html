Tap the button below to get your first story prompt!

HOW TO PLAY

One person says, “[Name]... tell me about the time...” and taps the button to finish the sentence.

The other person jumps into telling the story, fully committing to the idea!

The listener can ask questions along the way to help the story grow.

Feeling stuck?

Try this simple formula to get your story going:

One day I was ______ (where?)

Then I decided to ______ (doing what?)

When suddenly ______! (what happened?)

So I ______ (what did you do?)

And it was ______. (how did it end?)

Your story doesn't have to make sense or be clever. There are no wrong answers!
